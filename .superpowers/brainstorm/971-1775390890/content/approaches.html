Landing Page Approach — 3 Options

Each is optimized for Facebook ad → mobile → conversion. The difference is length and what you bet on to convince visitors.

A

Ultra-Short (1 screen)

Sections: Hero with result stats + WhatsApp CTA. That's it.


Pros

  • Maximum conversion speed — no scrolling needed
  • Perfect for impulse clicks from Facebook
  • Dead simple to build and A/B test

Cons

  • No social proof on page — relies 100% on the ad doing the convincing
  • Parents may want more info before trusting a stranger with their kid
  • No story, no results section
B

Focused (3-4 scrolls) ⭐ Recommended

Sections: Hero with urgency + Result bars (before/after scores) + Mini story with photo + 2-3 WhatsApp testimonial screenshots + WhatsApp CTA + Backup form


Pros

  • Enough proof to convince skeptical parents
  • Results section makes the impact visual and immediate
  • Personal story builds trust without being too long
  • WhatsApp screenshots feel authentic (not stock testimonials)
  • Still short enough for mobile attention spans

Cons

  • Slightly more scrolling before conversion
  • Requires good mobile flow to keep momentum
C

Full Page (current PPC pages, improved)

Sections: Hero + Benefits grid + Full story + Testimonials carousel + FAQ + Contact form


Pros

  • Comprehensive — answers every possible question
  • Good for retargeting ads (visitors who already saw the short version)
  • Can reuse your existing landing page components

Cons

  • Too long for cold Facebook traffic on mobile
  • Many visitors will bounce before reaching the CTA
  • Benefits grid and FAQ feel like padding for ad traffic that already saw the pitch

I recommend B (Focused) — it's the sweet spot between "convince me" and "don't bore me" for Facebook ad traffic. Click your choice and tell me in the terminal.
