Brand Name Options

Pick the ones that feel right. Think about how it sounds in a Facebook ad: "שיעורים פרטיים במתמטיקה עם [BRAND NAME]"

1

MathFlow

English, modern. Suggests smooth progress in math. Easy for Israelis to say and remember. Works well in a logo.

2

פיתוח — Pituach

Hebrew for "development/growth." Ties to personal growth, not just grades. Warm, local feel. פיתוח מתמטי as full version.

3

Matix

Short, punchy. "Mat" from math + modern "ix" ending. Sounds techy and professional. Easy to type, easy to remember.

4

מדרגה — Madrega

Hebrew for "step/level." Implies climbing up, grade by grade. Culturally resonant. "עולים מדרגה במתמטיקה" works as a tagline.

5

Sigma Math — סיגמא

The Greek summation symbol (Σ). Universally recognized as "math." Sounds professional. סיגמא — שיעורים פרטיים במתמטיקה.

6

Math+ (מאת' פלוס)

Dead simple. "Math" + the plus sign = improvement. Everyone gets it instantly. Clean in a logo. "מאת' פלוס — הציון שלך עולה".

7

ההפרש — HaHefresh

Hebrew for "the difference" — a math term (subtraction result). Double meaning: the difference in your grades + the difference Ben makes. "ההפרש — כי יש הפרש".
