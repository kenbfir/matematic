Design notes:

  • Layered bars — red "before" bar underneath, teal "after" bar on top. The contrast is instant.
  • Green badge — "+27 נקודות" makes the improvement scannable without reading the bars.
  • Real students — names and contexts from your actual testimonials.
  • Summary stat at bottom — one number that sticks: "+25 נקודות שיפור בממוצע"
  • On mobile — these cards stack vertically and are easy to scroll through quickly.
