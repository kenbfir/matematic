What's the real scarcity signal?

Must be credible and true — Israeli audience will see through fake urgency

A

Monthly Spots Limit

"אני מקבל עד 5 תלמידים חדשים בחודש — נשארו X מקומות למאי"

Credible because private tutors genuinely have limited hours. Update the number manually each month. Highest-converting scarcity type for solo tutors.

B

Exam Date Countdown

"מועד א׳ 2026 — נשארו X שבועות. כל שבוע שמפספסים = חומר שלא יכוסה"

Real, verifiable, exam-cycle tied. Works best for bagrut and academic pages. Less relevant for middle school.

C

Both — Spots + Exam Countdown

Spots limit on all pages + exam countdown on bagrut/academic pages only. Double urgency where it's real, single where it isn't.
