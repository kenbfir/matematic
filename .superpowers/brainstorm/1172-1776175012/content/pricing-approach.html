Should the landing pages show pricing?

This affects page structure and lead quality significantly

A

Show Trial Lesson Price Only

Display only the discounted trial lesson price (e.g., ₪99 / ₪120) as the entry point. Ongoing rates handled in conversation. Low barrier to click, price qualifies leads without anchoring ongoing rate.

Example: "שיעור ניסיון — ₪99 בלבד" with a clear CTA.

B

Show Full Package Pricing

Display trial lesson + ongoing package rates framed as outcomes ("מסלול בגרות — 8 שיעורים"). Pre-qualifies leads completely, fewer tire-kickers, higher close rate on calls.

Requires knowing the actual prices to put here.

C

No Pricing — Form + WhatsApp Only

No pricing on page. Call-to-action is purely "leave details / WhatsApp us." Price discussed after contact. More leads at top of funnel, more unqualified ones too.
