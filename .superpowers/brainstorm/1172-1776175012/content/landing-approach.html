Landing Page Approach

The pages at /bagrut, /middle-school, /academic already exist. How should we treat them?

A

Full Rebuild — Heavy PPC

Scrap the current structure. Build conversion machines: above-the-fold urgency, countdown timer, pricing section, guarantee badge, before/after stats, objection handling, sticky CTA bar. Every element exists to convert.

Best for

  • Paid ad traffic
  • High-intent visitors
  • A/B testing later

Trade-offs

  • More aggressive feel
  • More build time
B

Upgrade Existing — Add Key Sections

Keep the current hero + benefits + testimonials + form structure but add: urgency strip ("מועד א׳ 2026 — X שבועות"), pricing transparency, a guarantee section, and a sticky mobile CTA tuned per page.

Best for

  • Faster to ship
  • Keeps working parts
  • Lower risk

Trade-offs

  • Less differentiated per level
C

Pages Are Fine — Focus on Ads Only

The landing pages are good enough. Put all effort into creating polished, platform-specific ad creatives and copy for Google, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Yad2, and WhatsApp.

Best for

  • Fastest to launch ads
  • Test traffic before investing in pages

Trade-offs

  • Pages may leak conversions
